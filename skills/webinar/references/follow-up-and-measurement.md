# Follow-Up and Measurement

## Within 24 hours

| Segment | Message | Timing |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Engaged (asked a question, clicked the offer, stayed to the end) | Personal note referencing their moment, offer, and a booking link | Same day, from a person |
| Attended, no action | Replay, slides, and a resource tied to the offer | Within a few hours |
| Left early | Replay with timestamps for the parts they missed | Next morning |
| No-show | Replay and a three-bullet summary | Next morning |

Follow with one more touch 3 to 5 days later for everyone who did not act, then move them to normal nurture.

## Sales handoff

- Define hot-lead signals in advance: offer click, a pricing or buying question, stayed past the offer, matches ICP.
- Route hot leads to sales the same day with context: topic, questions asked, and engagement.
- Tag every registrant and attendee with the webinar as the source and the attendance status in the CRM.

## Replay and content recycling

- Publish the replay (gated or open, depending on the goal) and send it to the list.
- Cut 3 to 5 short clips, publish a written recap or blog post, and extract quotes for social.
- Pull the Q&A into an FAQ or a follow-up piece.
- Transcripts help the content get found and cited; publish one.

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| Registrations | Sign-ups by source |
| Show-up rate | Live attendees ÷ registrants |
| Hold rate | Attendees present at the offer ÷ attendees |
| Offer conversion | Offer clicks or bookings ÷ attendees |
| Meetings and pipeline | Opportunities created, and revenue influenced |
| Cost per attendee and per opportunity | Spend (including time) ÷ result |

Benchmarks vary by audience, price point, and list quality. Use published ranges only as a loose check and compare each event against your own prior events. For credit across touches, see **attribution**.

## Post-mortem

Within a week, record what worked: the title that pulled, the best channel, the drop-off minute, and the questions asked. Feed the questions into the next topic.
