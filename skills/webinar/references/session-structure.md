# Session Structure

## Run of show (45 minutes)

| Minutes | Segment | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| 0 to 3 | Open | Welcome, state the promise, say when the offer comes, ask a chat question |
| 3 to 28 | Teaching | 3 teachable points, each with proof (example, number, screenshot) |
| 28 to 30 | Bridge | Scripted: the product as the implementation of what was taught |
| 30 to 33 | Offer | One call to action, repeated in chat and on the final slide |
| 33 to 45 | Q&A | Seeded questions first, then live; end with the offer again |

For 30-minute sessions, cut to two teaching points. For workshops, replace teaching blocks with guided exercises.

## Roles

- **Presenter**: content and energy.
- **Moderator**: chat, polls, question selection, time checks.
- **Tech owner**: platform, audio, recording, backup link.

## Engagement tactics

- A poll or chat prompt in the first 2 minutes and again about every 7 minutes.
- Name attendees who ask questions.
- Switch between slides, live demo, and talking head to reset attention.
- Skip housekeeping at the start; put it in the confirmation email.

## Rehearsal checklist

- Full run-through with the real slides and screen share.
- Audio, camera, lighting, and internet backup tested at the same time of day.
- Offer link, polls, and handouts loaded and clicked through.
- Recording confirmed on. Backup presenter and a fallback dial-in or platform ready.
- Q&A seeds written down.

## Panels and interviews

Send guests the questions in advance, cap speakers at three plus a moderator, and give each a first and last question. Co-hosted sessions: agree on lead ownership in advance (see **co-marketing**).
